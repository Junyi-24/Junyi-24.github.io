Biography个人简介

I am a Ph.D. student in Network Security at the Institute for Network Sciences and Cyberspace (INSC), Tsinghua University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Yuanjie Li. 我是清华大学网络科学与网络空间研究院网络安全专业的博士研究生，导师为李元杰教授。

I received my bachelor's degree in Communication Engineering from the School of Information and Communication Engineering at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT) in 2024. 我于 2024 年在北京邮电大学信息与通信工程学院获得通信工程专业工学学士学位。

My research focuses on satellite networks. Currently, I am particularly interested in on-board AI and in-orbit computing. 我的研究聚焦卫星网络，目前对星上人工智能与在轨计算尤为感兴趣。
